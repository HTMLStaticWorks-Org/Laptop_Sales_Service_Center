Workstation engineering

Thermal Architecture & Sustained Clock Tuning

High-wattage mobile workstations suffer thermal throttling under prolonged renders and computations. Here is our calibrated tuning protocol.

01

Phase-Change Thermal Interface (PTM7950)

Replaces degraded stock paste with aerospace-grade phase-change compound, eliminating micro-voids at 45°C+ phase transition.

02

Dynamic V-F Curve & Undervolt Profiling

Fine-tunes the voltage-frequency curve in firmware, reducing heat dissipation by 8°C–14°C while maintaining 100% stable peak boost clocks.

03

Dual-Rank Low-Latency RAM Balancing

Upgrades matched JEDEC dual-channel kits with tightened sub-timings, preventing memory bus bottlenecks in 4K timelines and code compiling.

04

PCIe Gen4/5 Thermal Shielding & Striping

Installs high-conductive copper-graphene heatsinks over NVMe controllers to eliminate sustained write throttling during multi-GB transfers.
