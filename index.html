Rigorous standards

Lapserv 25-Point Diagnostic Standard

Every laptop serviced or refurbished through our workshop undergoes a multi-layer technical verification before handover.

Display & ColorPass

Visual & Color Calibration

Full sub-pixel dead-pixel mapping, back-light bleed uniformity inspection, and 100% sRGB color gamut checks.

  • Zero dead/stuck pixel tolerance
  • eDP panel cable integrity check
MotherboardPass

Power Rail & Telemetry

Oscilloscope and multimeter verification of 3.3V, 5V, and 12V rails with standby leak current measurement.

  • DC-in jack ripple voltage test
  • Charging IC & MOSFET thermals
ThermalsPass

Thermal & Acoustic Stress

30-minute dual CPU/GPU burn-in under heavy synthetics, measuring delta-T dissipation and acoustic fan decibels.

  • Vapor chamber vacuum pressure
  • Acoustic fan bearing test
I/O Ports & ChassisPass

Ports, Hinge & Input

Full 100-key switch matrix validation, USB-C PD load tests, Thunderbolt throughput, and calibrated hinge torque.

  • 100W USB-C Power Delivery test
  • Chassis grounding & isolation
Included with every completed repair or certified sale:

Printed Diagnostic Inspection Certificate with recorded serial numbers, battery health percentage, and warranty coverage stamp.
