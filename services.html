Common questions

Repair FAQs

How long will my repair take?

Timing depends on the diagnosis, parts availability and approved work. Confirm an estimate with Lapserv.

Will I receive a repair estimate first?

The intended process is to share the diagnosis and estimate and obtain approval before paid work begins.

Can I upgrade RAM or storage?

Upgrade options depend on the exact laptop model, supported memory and storage interfaces.

Do you guarantee liquid-damage recovery?

No recovery guarantee should be made. Liquid damage can affect multiple components and requires inspection.

Should I share my laptop password on this page?

No. Do not submit passwords or sensitive device credentials through a public enquiry form.
